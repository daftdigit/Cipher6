THEME:
How it works — your password runs through PBKDF2 (210,000 rounds, SHA-256) with a random salt to derive an AES-256 key. The message is sealed with AES-GCM using a random IV. Salt, IV, and ciphertext are packed together and shown as base64 — that whole block is what you share. Anyone decrypting it needs the exact password; there's no recovery if it's lost, and this tool keeps no copy of it. Clear All wipes the key field and both text panels in one click.
